## 1. Executive Summary & Context

As part of our continuous investment in workplace ergonomics, sustainable commuting, and flexible collaboration, we are transitioning our primary regional operations to the newly certified **Vlněna Eco-Park Facility (Building D)**. 

Effective **December 1, 2026**, our flexible working model introduces guaranteed core collaboration windows, enhanced home-office equipment stipends, and hot-desking reservations via our updated mobile portal.

---

## 2. Key Transition Milestones

The relocation and technical switch-over will take place in phased tranches to ensure zero interruption to client deliverables and production infrastructure.

| Phase | Milestone Description | Target Date | Impact Scope | Primary Lead |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 | Digital seat reservations open on Workplace portal | Nov 01, 2026 | All Employees | IT Operations |
| Phase 2 | Equipment packing crates delivered to legacy desks | Nov 15, 2026 | In-Office Staff | Facilities Mgmt |
| Phase 3 | Final data center hardware migration | Nov 27–29, 2026 | Core Engineering | NetOps Team |
| Phase 4 | Official ribbon-cutting & Welcome Breakfast at new hub | Dec 01, 2026 | All Staff | People Team |

---
